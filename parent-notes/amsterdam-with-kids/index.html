FAMILY TRAVEL / THE NETHERLANDS

Amsterdam with kids.
Six days in Haarlem & Amsterdam.

A local weekend, a city seen from the water, and room for the things the children remembered.

Travel notes by Yewon 23–28 October 2026 · Friday to Wednesday
6 daysHaarlem + AmsterdamWith childrenA weather-dependent coast option

Published 29 October 2026 · Operator information checked on the same date.

We flew from Exeter with KLM on Friday evening, spent the weekend in Haarlem, then moved into Amsterdam for canals, animals and art. The children loved the gorillas at ARTIS, a vegan lunch at the zoo, and finding ship models among the paintings at the Rijksmuseum.

A FAMILY TRIP FROM THE UK

You do not have to start in Exeter.

London, Bristol, Birmingham, Manchester, Edinburgh and other UK cities also have direct Amsterdam routes. We flew from Exeter; you can adapt the same Haarlem-and-Amsterdam plan around your own airport and flight times.

SATURDAY24 Oct

HAARLEM / A LOCAL WEEKEND

Market morning. Museum afternoon.

Morning marketLunchMuseum & walk

Saturday morning was for browsing the local market and having lunch: fried white fish and sandwiches. In the afternoon, we visited a local museum and walked around the neighbourhood.

Haarlem was part of the trip in its own right, not simply somewhere to sleep before Amsterdam. Oscar Desemcafé Bartel is one of the food spots linked in these notes.

A bookshop stop to add to the Haarlem walk: Catnap Books, an independent English-language bookshop with new and used books. It is a useful stop for families who read in English.

Market planning: the council lists Saturday markets at Grote Markt and Botermarkt. Check the market and opening times for your chosen day.

AN OPTIONAL COAST DAY / NOT VISITED

The beach day the weather changed.

Planned, then cancelledZandvoort aan ZeePack a picnic

We had planned to explore the surrounding area and take the train from Haarlem to the coast. Bad weather meant we did not make the beach trip, so I am keeping it here as a weather-dependent option rather than a first-hand review.

For a future Sunday outing, I would pack lunch, snacks and water rather than rely on finding a café when the children need to eat. Check a specific venue’s Sunday opening and weather-related changes before setting off; this is not a claim that every beach café is closed on Sundays.

The practical part: NS lists a direct journey of about 10 minutes between Haarlem and Zandvoort aan Zee. Recheck the service on your chosen day. For a separate dunes option near Haarlem, see Zuid-Kennemerland below. We have not walked a specific route there.

HAARLEM → AMSTERDAM

Start with the city from the water.

Move into Amsterdam75-minute cruiseDinner out

On Monday we moved into Amsterdam, with Stayokay Amsterdam Vondelpark as our city base. Our first outing was a 75-minute Blue Boat canal cruise, seeing the city from the water, followed by dinner out in the evening.

We also bought a 24-hour transport ticket for our city exploring. Gifu was another food stop from our trip; you can find the restaurant details below.

Boat booking: Blue Boat uses two boarding points. The map we shared points to the jetty near the Hard Rock Café, but always check the address on your own ticket.

TUESDAY27 Oct

ARTIS / OUR LONGEST SINGLE STOP

Give the zoo room in the day.

About 5 hours for usGorillasVegan lunch

We spent about five hours at ARTIS. The children enjoyed the gorillas and discovering animals they do not often get to see. I also came away with a positive personal impression of the animals’ condition.

Our vegan lunch at the zoo became one of the memories the children kept talking about. It was the combination of animals and a different food experience that stayed with them.

Our planning note: five hours was our family’s visit length, not a fixed time everyone needs. We would leave this as the main outing of the day rather than assume it can fit into a short gap.

WEDNESDAY28 Oct

RIJKSMUSEUM → AIRPORT

Paintings, ship models, then home.

Final dayRijksmuseumAirport afterwards

On our last day, we visited the Rijksmuseum before heading to the airport. The children enjoyed the paintings, but the ship models were also a way into the museum for them.

Before copying this day: work backwards from your own flight, allow for the airport journey and check-in, and arrange luggage storage before visiting. Do not assume the museum will store travel cases. A museum visit on departure day only works if your flight leaves enough time.

THE PRACTICAL NOTES

Before you book.

Check current admission prices, room inclusions and transport coverage directly. We stayed with friends in Haarlem, so this itinerary is not an all-hotel cost comparison or a quoted family-trip total.

A 24-hour ticket is not every train.

If you choose a GVB day ticket, it runs for 24 hours from first check-in and covers GVB trams, buses and metro. It does not cover NS trains to Haarlem or Zandvoort. This guidance applies to the GVB product, not every ticket sold for travel around Amsterdam.

GVB ticket guidance ↗

Pack for the coast, not just the forecast.

For the optional beach day, I would bring lunch, snacks, water and suitable layers. Check local weather and transport before leaving, and do not rely on finding an open café exactly where you finish walking.

Keep departure day flexible.

Check luggage arrangements, your museum start time, the airport journey and your airline’s deadlines before combining the Rijksmuseum with a flight home.
