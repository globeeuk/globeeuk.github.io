ExeterTicketed

Exeter's Underground Passages

Explore the exhibition and follow a guide beneath the city through medieval water passages.

When
Term time: Thursday–Friday and Sunday 11am–4pm; Saturday 10.30am–4.30pm (subject to change); Monday–Wednesday closed. Summer holidays: Wednesday–Sunday 11am–4pm. Closed 24–25 December.
Where
2 Paris Street, Exeter, Devon, EX1 1GA
Cost
Ticketed: adult £9; concession £7; child 5–17 £6; family up to 2 adults and 3 children £25.
Age guidance
Tours: ages 5+; children aged 5–17 must be accompanied by an adult.
Booking
Advance booking advised at weekends and during school holidays. Arrive 15 minutes before your tour.
  • Under-5s may visit the exhibition free with an adult, but cannot join the tour.
  • Wheelchairs and pushchairs can access the exhibition only. Passages are narrow and low; unsuitable for claustrophobia.
