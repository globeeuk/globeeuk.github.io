Heritage Open Days at Killerton

Visit Killerton's house, gardens and historic laundry during its free-entry weekend near Exeter.

When
Sunday 13 September 2026, 10:00–17:00
Where
Killerton, Broadclyst, Exeter, Devon, EX5 3LE
Cost
Free admission. Non-member parking: £2.60 for up to two hours or £5.20 all day; members park free.
Age guidance
Children welcome; no numerical age restriction is stated.
Booking
No advance booking required. Collect an entry ticket at Visitor Reception.
  • On Sunday 13 September, the house closes at 4pm with last entry at 3.30pm. The garden closes at 5pm.
  • The property lists Hill Fort play-area works for 5–16 October.
  • The gardens include slopes and steep sections; dress for the weather.
