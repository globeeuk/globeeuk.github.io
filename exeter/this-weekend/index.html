Darts Farm Pumpkin Fest 2026

Explore the pumpkin displays, family entertainment and seasonal food at Darts Farm in Topsham.

When
Saturday 31 October 2026, 10:00–16:30
Where
Darts Farm, Topsham, Devon EX3 0QH
Cost
£5 per adult or child; under-1s free. Pumpkins and some activities cost extra.
Age guidance
Family event; under-1s enter free. No numerical upper or lower age limit is published.
Booking
Advance booking of an arrival slot is recommended. Walk-ins may be admitted, but entry is not guaranteed. Last entry is 3pm; parking is free and payment is card only.
  • Last entry is 3pm although the event closes at 4.30pm.
  • Pumpkin purchases and some activities cost extra.
  • Dogs must be on leads; free parking is listed.

Halloween Spook-Fest – Crealy

Join Crealy’s family Halloween programme with themed mazes, shows and rides near Exeter.

When
Saturday 31 October 2026, 10:00–17:00
Where
Crealy Theme Park & Resort, Sidmouth Road, Clyst St Mary, Devon EX5 1DR
Cost
Online tickets from £20 per person; gate price £25 per person; children under 92cm free. A pumpkin for each child is included during Spook-Fest (24–31 October). Optional upgrades cost extra; check the date-specific final total.
Age guidance
Family event; a gentler trick-or-treat experience is aimed at under-10s. Individual rides and attractions have age or height restrictions.
Booking
Advance online booking offers the advertised lower price; gate tickets are listed. Check date-specific availability and ride restrictions.
  • Individual attractions have their own age and height rules.
  • The final October run continues through Saturday 31 October; the separate free-return offer on 1 November has eligibility terms and is not listed here as a general event date.
  • For 25 and 31 October the park calendar lists 10am–5pm, last entry 4pm. The separate 24 October weather exception was a 4pm close and 3pm last entry; it is not a change to the later dates.
