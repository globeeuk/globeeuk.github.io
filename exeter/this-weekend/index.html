Lower Dean, near BuckfastleighTicketed

Pennywell Pumpkin Festival

Explore the pumpkin patch, trail and games during a daytime visit to Pennywell Farm.

When
Saturday 26 September 2026, 09:00–16:00; Sunday 27 September 2026, 09:00–16:00
Where
Pennywell Farm, Lower Dean, Buckfastleigh, Devon TQ11 0LT
Cost
For 26–27 September: adult 16+ £24.95; child over 90cm £19.95; concession £21.95; infant below 90cm free. Check the final booking total. Pumpkin purchases, marshmallows, food and drink cost extra.
Age guidance
Family-friendly; numerical age range TBC. Children need adult supervision; individual rides have age or height restrictions.
Booking
All visitors must book online in advance, including free infants and season-pass holders. Availability may change.
  • Daytime festival activities are included with farm admission; taking a pumpkin home costs extra.
  • Free parking. Some outdoor paths are uneven; an all-terrain pushchair is recommended.
  • This listing is for the daytime festival. Pumpkin Nights is a separate evening event.
