Exeter Library

Choose books together in the children's section overlooking Rougemont Gardens. Reading and borrowing books are free.

When
Operator-published hours: Monday and Friday 9am–6pm; Tuesday and Thursday 9am–7pm; Wednesday 10am–6pm; Saturday 9am–5pm; Sunday noon–4pm. Check exceptional closures before travelling.
Where
Castle Street, Exeter, Devon, EX4 3PQ
Cost
Free to read books; joining and borrowing books are also free. Individual events may have separate charges.
Age guidance
Children of all ages, including babies, are welcome.
Booking
Reading-visit booking requirement: TBC. Check event listings before attending a scheduled activity.

What counts as free here?

The listed activity or general admission must be free. A paid children’s activity with free accompanying adults does not qualify. Optional extras and parking are listed separately when the provider publishes them.
