Norway Spruce · Premium
£40 · Traditional pine scent and short, prickly needles. A lower-cost choice for putting the tree up in mid-December.
Nordmann Fir · Standard
£45 · Soft needles that hold well. A fuller tree and a practical choice if you decorate early.

Cut, pot-grown or potted?

Cut: a seasonal tree without roots. Pot-grown: raised in a container with its root system; ask about care and moving it outdoors afterwards. Potted: may have been dug up and put in a pot — the label alone does not mean pot-grown or guarantee survival.

Hoping to rent a living tree?

We have not confirmed a rental service delivering to Exeter for 2026. A pot-grown tree is a purchase, not a rental: ask the supplier about care and whether it can stay outside after Christmas.

Check access, parking and ground conditions with each farm before travelling with a pushchair or anyone with limited mobility.
