Whimple · East DevonBook a vehicle slot

Cotley Farm Christmas

Reindeer and farm animals, a Christmas tree barn and a stop for hot chocolate. A tree-shopping trip with a little more to see.

When
Sat 21 November, then Fri 27 November–Thu 24 December 2026. Weekdays 10am–5.30pm; weekends 10am–6.30pm. Until 8pm on 27 November and 4 December; closes at noon on Christmas Eve.
Where
Cotley Farm, Whimple, Exeter, EX5 2QR.
Cost
£6.50–£10 per car, depending on the date. Entry is refunded if you buy a Christmas tree. Trees, food and drinks cost extra.
Booking
A vehicle time slot must be booked online before visiting. Check the organiser for current availability.
Age guidance
Family visits; exact age guidance TBC.

Mum’s note: trees, animals and a warm drink in one visit.

Exe Valley Christmas Trees

Pick a tree from the field or browse pot-grown trees and the festive barn. Bring wellies for a wander among the trees.

When
Wed 25 November–Wed 23 December 2026. Monday–Friday and Sunday 9am–5pm; Saturday 8.30am–5pm.
Where
Canns Farm, Thorverton, Exeter, EX5 5PJ.
Cost
Trees are purchased separately; price varies by size and variety. Admission charge TBC — check with the farm.
Booking
No visit time slot needed. Trees cannot be tagged or reserved ahead of a visit. The farm suggests weekdays to avoid queues.
Age guidance
Exact age guidance TBC.

Mum’s note: a simple tree-picking outing, with pot-grown options. The Father Christmas visit date is still TBC.

Hoping to rent a living tree?

We have not confirmed a rental service delivering to Exeter for 2026. A pot-grown tree is a purchase, not a rental: ask the supplier about care and whether it can stay outside after Christmas.

Check access, parking and ground conditions with each farm before travelling with a pushchair or anyone with limited mobility.
