Pumpkin Patch – Avon Valley

Pick pumpkins and follow the Big Boo Trail on Avon Valley’s outdoor patch near Keynsham.

When
Saturday 10 October 2026, 10:00–17:00; Sunday 11 October 2026, 10:00–17:00; Saturday 17 October 2026, 10:00–17:00; Sunday 18 October 2026, 10:00–17:00
Where
Avon Valley Adventure Park, Pixash Lane, Bath Road, Keynsham, Bristol BS31 1TP
Cost
Ticketed; exact 2026 patch-entry price TBC. Pumpkins, optional rides and stalls cost extra. Check the final booking total; on-the-day tickets carry a surcharge.
Age guidance
Family activity for all ages; supervise children throughout. Numerical age limits TBC.
Booking
Book in advance to avoid disappointment; the operator says this event can sell out. On-the-day tickets, if available, carry a surcharge.
  • Pumpkins, rides and side stalls are not included in patch entry; the ticket does not include access to Avon Valley Adventure Park.
  • The operator advises boots or wellies and says the farmland patch is unsuitable for wheelchairs or pushchairs.
  • Dates and hours here describe the daytime Pumpkin Patch, not the separate Pumpkin Patch Nights event.

The Great Bristol Ball Run

Send a wooden ball through six giant outdoor runs across Millennium Square and Anchor Square.

When
Saturday 10 October 2026; Sunday 11 October 2026; Saturday 17 October 2026; Sunday 18 October 2026
Where
Millennium Square and Anchor Square, Bristol, BS1 5DB
Cost
£6 for a wooden ball, yours to keep and reuse on repeat visits.
Age guidance
Family activity; numerical age range TBC.
Booking
No advance booking requirement is stated; balls are sold from the We The Curious shop.
  • Keep the wooden ball to reuse on all six runs and on repeat visits.
  • The shop normally opens 10am–5pm on Saturdays and Sundays. These are ball-collection hours; separate outdoor activity hours remain TBC.
  • The organiser labels this outdoor activity as partially accessible; check access for your family.
