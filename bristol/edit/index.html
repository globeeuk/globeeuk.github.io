Bristol Harbour Railway train rides

Take a steam train ride along Bristol Harbour Railway from M Shed towards the SS Great Britain.

When
Saturday 26 September 2026, 11:00–17:00; Sunday 27 September 2026, 11:00–17:00
Where
M Shed, Princes Wharf, Wapping Road, Bristol, BS1 4RN
Cost
Single £3.50 or return £5; children under six travel free. Entry to M Shed is free; donations welcome.
Age guidance
Family activity; numerical age range TBC.
Booking
Buy tickets on the day from the ticket office by the M Shed platform; rides are subject to availability.
  • Trips last approximately 15 minutes. Buy tickets at the ticket office by the M Shed platform.
  • Check the official page on the day because weather, mechanical issues or crew availability can alter the service.
  • Children under six travel free; dogs are welcome.

Pick up a free detective map and hunt for tiny Aardman treasures around Bristol Museum & Art Gallery.

When
Saturday 26 September 2026, 10:00–17:00; Sunday 27 September 2026, 10:00–17:00; Saturday 3 October 2026, 10:00–17:00; Sunday 4 October 2026, 10:00–17:00
Where
Bristol Museum & Art Gallery, Queens Road, Bristol, BS8 1RL
Cost
Free trail and free museum entry; donations welcome.
Age guidance
Families and curious explorers of all ages
Booking
No booking required for an ordinary family visit. Groups of 10 or more must book in advance.

The Great Bristol Ball Run

Send a wooden ball through six giant outdoor runs across Millennium Square and Anchor Square.

When
Saturday 26 September 2026; Sunday 27 September 2026; Saturday 3 October 2026; Sunday 4 October 2026
Where
Millennium Square and Anchor Square, Bristol, BS1 5DB
Cost
£6 for a wooden ball, yours to keep and reuse on repeat visits.
Age guidance
Family activity; numerical age range TBC.
Booking
No advance booking requirement is stated; balls are sold from the We The Curious shop.
  • Keep the wooden ball to reuse on all six runs and on repeat visits.
  • The shop normally opens 10am–5pm on Saturdays and Sundays. These are ball-collection hours; separate outdoor activity hours remain TBC.
  • The organiser labels this outdoor activity as partially accessible; check access for your family.

Visit the Villa – Kings Weston Roman Villa

Explore Roman mosaic floors, underfloor heating and a bath suite, with soldiers, crafts, dressing up and family trails.

When
Saturday 26 September 2026, 10:00–14:00
Where
Kings Weston Roman Villa, Long Cross, Bristol, BS11 0LH
Cost
Free; donations welcome.
Age guidance
Family activity; numerical age range TBC.
Booking
Drop in; no booking required for this open day. School and organised group visits must be arranged in advance.
  • The event includes Roman soldiers, craft activities, dressing up and trails.
  • Expect uneven surfaces, grassy areas and low walls.
  • The organiser mentions parking on surrounding residential streets and buses 3 or 4 stopping on Long Cross.
