Bristol on Ice – Harbourside Ice Rink

An ice rink at the Harbourside Christmas event, with rides and food stalls nearby.

When
Saturday 28 November 2026, 12:00–22:00; Sunday 29 November 2026, 12:00–22:00
Where
Amphitheatre, Bristol Harbour, Bristol
Cost
Free site entry. Skating: child £12.95, adult £14.95 or family (two adults and two children) £49.95. Skate aid £5.
Age guidance
Skaters must be 5 or older. Under-15s need an accompanying adult. Ask which ticket to select for age 17, which appears in both price bands.
Booking
Advance online booking is recommended for skating. Free site entry needs no ticket. Slot availability is TBC.
  • Rink cover is TBC. The organiser points to Millennium Square Car Park, BS1 5LL. Check parking charges separately.
  • The festival runs to 27 December, with closures on 30 November and Christmas Day.
