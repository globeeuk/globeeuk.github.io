Docks Heritage Weekend – M Shed

Explore Bristol's working harbour heritage with a quiz trail, demonstrations, quayside theatre and a Saturday children's workshop.

When
Saturday 19 September 2026 – Sunday 20 September 2026, 10:00–17:00
Where
M Shed, Princes Wharf, Bristol, BS1 4RN
Cost
Free museum entry and core programme. Ticket charges apply to boat trips and crane visits.
Age guidance
Age TBC; Saturday includes paper-boat making and colouring for children
Booking
No booking is stated for the core weekend programme. Paid boat trips and crane visits are subject to availability; check their official listings.
  • Saturday includes the children's paper-boat and colouring workshop; Sunday includes historic Port of Bristol films.
  • Working exhibits can change because of weather, mechanical issues or volunteer availability.

Clifton, BristolFree admission

Mini-museum hunt! 50 years of Aardman

Pick up a free detective map and hunt for tiny Aardman treasures around Bristol Museum & Art Gallery.

When
Saturday 19 September 2026, 10:00–17:00; Sunday 20 September 2026, 10:00–17:00
Where
Bristol Museum & Art Gallery, Queens Road, Bristol, BS8 1RL
Cost
Free trail and free museum entry; donations welcome.
Age guidance
Families and curious explorers of all ages
Booking
No booking required for an ordinary family visit. Groups of 10 or more must book in advance.
  • This free trail is at Bristol Museum & Art Gallery, not the sold-out paid Aardman exhibition at M Shed.
  • The British and World Wildlife Galleries are closed until summer 2027, but the museum and trail remain open.

Bristol HarboursideFree admission

Play The Bluey Way

Meet Bluey, make a Barky Boat and see a new mural at this free family event in Millennium Square.

When
Saturday 19 September 2026, 12:30–18:30
Where
Millennium Square, 1 Anchor Road, Bristol, BS1 5DB
Cost
Free. Each child needs a ticket; adults do not need tickets.
Age guidance
Best suited to ages 2–10; no strict age limit
Booking
Booking essential. A limited number of same-day tickets may be available, but this is not guaranteed.
  • Bring water shoes or wellington boots, plus a change of clothes for the Barky Boat water activity.
  • Allow for a possible queue; the experience itself lasts about 15–30 minutes.
