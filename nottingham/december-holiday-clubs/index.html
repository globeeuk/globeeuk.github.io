How we chose these examples.

We checked the organiser’s own 2026 winter notice or booking calendar, then looked for clear dates, location, age guidance, costs and a route to book. The first cards add a mix of local areas and activities; the table gives you more choices. We have not compared popularity or inspected these clubs in person.

  • Choose an actual session date; a run of dates does not mean every day is available.
  • Funded HAF needs eligibility checks. Paid places, longer days and extras may cost more.
  • Confirm collection, meals, allergies and any support needs with the provider. TBC means we could not confirm that detail.
