Still curious. The story of Globee.

Press play to begin, or watch on Instagram using the link below.

View this film on Instagram ↗@globee.uk ↗
Read the film transcript

Lately, I've been looking back. At the places I've been, the work I've done, and what I've learned. Now, as a mum of two, I wonder how I could use it all. For myself, my children, and other families.

I was a curious child. And that curiosity has stayed with me. I travelled, took notes, and wrote a backpacking guidebook. I also worked in tech for over eight years, organising information and sharing ideas.

Now, in Britain, I share that curiosity with my two children. On days out, we find a fossil, then look it up together at home. Music or swimming leads us to a class to try. Our discoveries become learning. And learning brings us new things to enjoy.

Our family notebook filled with places, classes and discoveries. I wanted to share it, and learn from other families, too. That's how Globee began.

Dear fellow parent. They say it takes a village to raise a child. I hope Globee can help us on that journey, for my children, and for all our children. With love, Yewon.
