Privacy & analytics

Updated 24 September 2026

Cookie-free basic statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics by default to count visits and page views and understand page performance, solely to improve Globee. It does not use analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. Counts are visits and page views, not a count of unique people. These statistics appear in Cloudflare, separately from Google Analytics.

Cloudflare receives the page address without query strings or fragments, the referring page without query strings, and technical information such as browser, device, country and page timings. Network requests also expose an IP address to the receiving service; “cookie-free” does not mean no information is processed. Read Cloudflare’s analytics information and privacy policy.

You can stop basic statistics using the control near the bottom of any page, without charge. The page reloads to apply the choice, which is remembered on this browser. Earlier recorded analytics refusals are preserved when introducing this service. A Global Privacy Control signal also disables basic statistics. We do not use these statistics for advertising or profiling.

Optional Google Analytics cookies

Globee uses optional Google Analytics 4 cookies to understand how the directory is used and improve it. Google Analytics only loads after you choose “Allow analytics”. If you choose “No thanks” or leave the choice unanswered, no Google Analytics requests are sent.

When allowed, analytics measures visits and actions such as switching between the list and map or following provider links. Analytics cookies identify a browser and connect visits into sessions. Google also receives technical information needed to process the requests. These are not anonymous measurements.

We do not send search text, selected child ages, date filters, user IDs or the full referring page in our configured measurements. Advertising consent remains denied, and Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled. Read Google’s privacy information.

Your analytics choice

Checking your analytics setting…

Use Analytics settings on any page to allow or stop Google Analytics cookies. This choice is separate from cookie-free basic statistics. Your choice is saved on this browser. A previous “No thanks” or “Stop site analytics” choice is kept. Withdrawal stops future Google Analytics measurement and removes this site’s analytics cookies; it does not erase data already collected. All directory features work without analytics. Local previews do not send analytics.

Directory data and local storage

The public directory reads our published Google Sheet. A recent copy is stored in your browser to reduce loading on slower connections. If you stop analytics, that preference is also stored in your browser. No account or child profile is needed.

Maps, photographs and links

Opening the map loads Leaflet and OpenStreetMap map images. Some photographs and fonts are delivered by external providers, which receive the normal network information needed to serve them. Location pins are approximate; check the provider for the exact entrance.

Official provider links and our questions and tips Google Form open external websites with their own privacy notices. Do not put children’s names or sensitive personal information in a public tip. To contact the Globee editor, use the questions and tips form.

Read OpenStreetMap’s attribution and information.
