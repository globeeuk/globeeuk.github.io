Privacy & analytics

Updated 13 September 2026

Site improvement statistics

Globee uses Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how the directory is used and where it needs improving. Cookieless measurement starts when you visit the live site unless you stop it below. Analytics storage and advertising storage remain denied, so Globee does not use Google Analytics cookies. Any old Google Analytics cookies from the previous setup are removed.

We measure page visits, list and map switches, editorial selections, results pages and visits to provider websites. The measurement may also include the time of the visit, browser and device type, the referring website’s origin and normal network information used to deliver the service.

We do not send your search text, selected child ages, date filters, user IDs or full referring page. We do not use the data for advertising, personalisation, profiling or linking activity across websites. Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled in the website configuration.

Google provides this measurement service on Globee’s behalf. Read Google’s privacy information. This setup is intended for the statistical-purpose exception described in the ICO guidance on storage and access technologies.

Your analytics choice

Checking your analytics setting…

Your choice is saved on this browser. A previous “No thanks” choice is kept. You can use every part of Globee after stopping analytics. Local previews do not send analytics.

Directory data and local storage

The public directory reads our published Google Sheet. A recent copy is stored in your browser to reduce loading on slower connections. If you stop analytics, that preference is also stored in your browser. No account or child profile is needed.

Maps, photographs and links

Opening the map loads Leaflet and OpenStreetMap map images. Some photographs and fonts are delivered by external providers, which receive the normal network information needed to serve them. Location pins are approximate; check the provider for the exact entrance.

Official provider links and our questions and tips Google Form open external websites with their own privacy notices. Do not put children’s names or sensitive personal information in a public tip. To contact the Globee editor, use the questions and tips form.

Read OpenStreetMap’s attribution and information.
