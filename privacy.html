Privacy & cookies

Updated 10 September 2026

Optional analytics

Globee uses Google Analytics 4 only if you choose Allow analytics. It helps us understand page visits, list/map use, editorial selections and clicks to providers. Google may process browser, device and network information as part of providing this service.

We do not deliberately send your search text, selected child ages or date filters as analytics parameters. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our website configuration. Google Analytics cookies may recognise your browser across visits.

You can decline and still use the directory. You can change your choice here or through Cookie settings in the site footer. Local previews do not send analytics.

Optional analytics. Allow Google Analytics cookies to help us improve Globee. You can browse without them.

Directory data and local storage

The public directory reads our published Google Sheet. A recent copy is stored in your browser to reduce loading on slower connections. Your analytics preference is also saved in your browser. No account or child profile is needed.

Maps, photographs and links

Opening the map loads Leaflet and OpenStreetMap map images. Some photographs and fonts are delivered by external providers, which receive the normal network information needed to serve them. Location pins are approximate; check the provider for the exact entrance.

Official provider links and our questions/tips Google Form open external websites with their own privacy notices. Do not put children’s names or sensitive personal information in a public tip. To contact the Globee editor, use the questions and tips form.

Read Google’s privacy information and OpenStreetMap’s attribution and information.
